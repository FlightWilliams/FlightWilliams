Lantern Oath / 灯誓牌
Author: Will Wang

The setting, card names and descriptions, character dialogue, coin faces, lantern images and illustrated card portraits were authored for this project. The Night Watch, Ellie's Echo and Lighthouse Warden belong to this original mystery: duty logs, a shared lantern and records of an evacuation. The card paintings were generated for this project with OpenAI's image generation tool and original character briefs. They use a realistic Western fantasy oil-illustration style: visible brushwork, natural light, individual adult faces, practical work clothes, recognizable animals and everyday objects. These are original illustrations, not film stills or portraits of actors. Every ordinary card has a separate painted atlas cell; the six recurring leads retain their defining features, and ordinary and gold editions share the same painting. The square paintings retain their authored colors and are not recolored by chapter themes or foil effects. Small summoned tokens can still use the project's deterministic SVG illustrations. Display names and descriptions avoid corpse imagery; stable internal identifiers remain compatible with existing saves.
Music uses thirty independently arranged original detective instrumentals and local procedural synthesis: picked guitar, warm piano and electric piano, upright bass and brushed percussion. Home and result cues share the same restrained tone. Sound effects share its recorded samples and synthesized cues. Paper and most combat recordings have CC0 sources; the adapted fire recording is distributed under CC-BY-SA 3.0. Attribution, source details, the complete license and independent WAVs are in audio/. Eleven legacy MP3 samples have no per-sample provenance record in the existing project and are not represented as CC0 or commercially cleared.

The handwritten signature uses a subset of Dancing Script, renamed Will Wang Signature, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright 2016 The Dancing Script Project Authors. The complete notice is distributed in assets/font-OFL.txt and embedded with the stylesheet.
