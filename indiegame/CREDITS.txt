Lantern Oath / 灯誓牌
Author: Will Wang

The setting, card names and descriptions, character dialogue, coin faces, lantern images and geometric pact emblems were authored for this project.
Music uses original note scores and local procedural synthesis. All sound effects are generated from oscillators and noise; this edition contains no recorded sound samples.

The handwritten signature uses a subset of Dancing Script, renamed Will Wang Signature, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Copyright 2016 The Dancing Script Project Authors. The complete notice is distributed in assets/font-OFL.txt and embedded with the stylesheet.
